Privacy Policy

Smart Bus (website and Android app "Smart Bus", package in.smartbus.app) · Last updated: 28 November 2026

Smart Bus helps a college plan its buses from students' daily ride choices, collect transport fees and show students where their bus is. This policy explains what data the service uses and why. The college that uses Smart Bus decides how its students' data is used; Smart Bus processes that data on the college's behalf.

Data we use

DataWhoseWhy
Student ID, name, designated bus stop, transport-fee statusStudents Read from the college's records to verify registration, plan routes and allow seat booking.
Password (stored only as a salted hash)All usersTo log in.
Daily "Yes / No" ride choiceStudentsTo count riders per stop and assign seats.
Bus and seat assignmentStudentsTo show each student their bus.
Precise location (GPS)Drivers only, while they share a trip To show students where their bus is and estimate arrival times. Students' location is never collected.
Fee payments: amount, date, method, gateway referenceStudents who pay online To record the payment and mark the fee as paid. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's checkout and are never seen or stored by Smart Bus.
Mobile numberStudents who pay onlineCollected by Razorpay during checkout, as required for payments.

Who we share it with

  • The college (transport office and administrators) sees ride choices, assignments, bus locations and fee payments.
  • Razorpay processes online payments under its own privacy policy (razorpay.com/privacy).
  • Hosting and routing providers run the servers and calculate road routes from bus-stop coordinates (no personal data is sent for routing).

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Account and payment records are kept for as long as the college uses the service and as required for its accounting. Bus GPS history is kept only as long as needed for trip records. The college can ask for data to be deleted.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. Payments are verified with the payment gateway's cryptographic signature before a fee is marked as paid.

Your choices and rights

You can stop using the app at any time. Drivers can stop sharing location at any time from the driver screen, and can deny location permission in Android settings. To see, correct or delete your data, contact your college transport office, which will pass the request to us. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may also withdraw consent and raise grievances through the college.

Children

Smart Bus is intended for college students and staff. It is not designed for children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and change the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact your college transport office, or the Smart Bus team through the project page at github.com/hemanthreddy243/AI_Transportation_College_System.
